Verdict: needs_human confidence low

The alert looks like a scheduled backup. HarborBackup.exe ran as svc-backup under services.exe with "--job nightly" and uploaded over 443 to vault.offsite-backup.example (198.51.100.140). I found no malicious indicators, but I also found no baseline: no earlier runs of this process, no earlier traffic to that domain, and no upload size in the data. Someone needs to ask the client whether this backup product and offsite vault are authorized, and whether WS-02 should run backups at this time.
