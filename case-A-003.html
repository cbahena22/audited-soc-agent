Verdict: needs_human confidence medium

At about 02:44 UTC, it-ops2 logged on to WS-07 over RDP from 192.0.2.21. About two minutes later they used mmc.exe and net.exe to add user07 to local Administrators. The same account and source IP did the same thing on SRV-01 at about 02:13, adding svc-backup. The pattern is consistent with either an IT admin doing planned work or a compromised admin account. The logs don't show a change ticket or any malware or C2 activity. Someone needs to ask the client whether it-ops2 made these changes and whether they were authorized.
